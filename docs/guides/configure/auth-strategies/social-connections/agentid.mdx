Enabling OAuth with [AgentID](https://www.agentid.com/) allows your users to sign up and sign in to your Clerk application with their AgentID account.

## Configure for your development instance

For _development instances_, Clerk uses preconfigured shared OAuth credentials and redirect URIs—no other configuration is needed.

1. In the Clerk Dashboard, navigate to the [**SSO connections**](https://dashboard.clerk.com/~/user-authentication/sso-connections) page.
1. Select **Add connection** and select **For all users**.
1. Select **AgentID** from the provider list.

## Configure for your production instance

For _production instances_, you must provide custom credentials.

To make the setup process easier, it's recommended to keep two browser tabs open: one for the [Clerk Dashboard](https://dashboard.clerk.com/~/user-authentication/sso-connections) and one for the [AgentID console](https://console.agentid.com/).

<Steps>
  ### Enable AgentID as a social connection in Clerk

  1. In the Clerk Dashboard, navigate to the [**SSO connections**](https://dashboard.clerk.com/~/user-authentication/sso-connections) page.
  1. Select **Add connection** and select **For all users**.
  1. Select **AgentID** from the provider list.
  1. Ensure that both **Enable for sign-up and sign-in** and **Use custom credentials** are toggled on.
  1. Save the **Redirect URL** somewhere secure. Keep the page open.

  ### Register a client in the AgentID console

  1. In the [AgentID console](https://console.agentid.com/), select **Register client**.
  1. Complete the form as follows:
     - Under **App name**, enter the name of your app.
     - Under **Redirect URI**, paste the **Redirect URL** value you saved from the Clerk Dashboard.
  1. Select **Register client**. You'll be redirected to your client's page.
  1. Save the **Client ID** and **Client secret** values somewhere secure. The client secret is shown only once.

  > [!NOTE]
  > Clerk requests the `openid`, `email`, and `profile` scopes from AgentID. AgentID also exposes the `owner_profile` and `owner_email` scopes, which return the name and email address of the person who owns the agent. To request them, add them to the **Scopes** field on the connection's configuration page.
