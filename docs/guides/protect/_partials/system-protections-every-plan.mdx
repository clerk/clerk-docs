| Protection | What it does | Applies to | Action |
| - | - | - | - |
| [Challenge suspicious sign-ups](/docs/guides/protect/bot-protection) | Challenges sign-ups that look automated or come through VPNs, Tor, or proxies. | Sign-ups | Challenge |
| [Require Device Intelligence checks](/docs/guides/protect/protections/require-device-intelligence) | Blocks a sign-up created by a browser that has no verified Device Intelligence result. | Sign-ups | Block |
| [Lock accounts after failed sign-ins](/docs/guides/protect/user-lockout) | Locks an account after too many failed sign-in attempts. | Sign-ins | Lock account |
| [Device Trust](/docs/guides/protect/device-trust) | Asks for a second factor when a user signs in with a password on a device Clerk doesn't recognize. | Password sign-ins | Require second factor |
| [User enumeration protection](/docs/guides/protect/user-enumeration-protection) | Makes it harder to find out which email addresses and phone numbers have an account. | Sign-ups, sign-ins, and profile updates | Rate limit, or hide whether an account exists |
| [Block email subaddresses](/docs/guides/protect/protections/block-email-subaddresses) | Rejects an email address that adds a subaddress to one that already has an account, such as `user+1@example.com`. | Sign-ups and adding an email address | Reject email address |
| [Block disposable email addresses](/docs/guides/protect/protections/block-disposable-email-addresses) | Rejects email addresses known to be disposable. | Sign-ups and adding an email address | Reject email address |
