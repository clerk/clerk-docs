<Properties>
  - `userProfileDestination(_:)`
  - `@ViewBuilder (Route) -> some View`

  Builds the view to push when the user selects a custom row. The closure receives the selected row's `route`.

  ---

  - `userProfileOAuthConfig(_:)`
  - [`[OAuthProviderConfig]`](#oauthproviderconfig)

  Requests additional OAuth scopes or prompts per provider when the user connects or reconnects a social account. When an existing connected account hasn't approved every configured scope, `UserProfileView` offers to reconnect it. See [Request additional OAuth scopes after sign-up](/docs/guides/configure/auth-strategies/social-connections/overview#request-additional-oauth-scopes-after-sign-up) for an example.

  ---

  - `userProfileRows(_:)`
  - [`[UserProfileCustomRow<Route>]`](#userprofilecustomrow)

  Custom rows to show on the root profile screen, alongside Clerk's built-in rows. Rows that share a placement show in array order.
</Properties>

### `UserProfileCustomRow`

<Properties>
  - `icon`
  - `UserProfileRowIcon`

  The row's icon. Pass `.system(name:)` for an SF Symbol, or `.asset(name:width:height:)` for an image in your app's asset catalog. Clerk renders asset icons as templates, tinted the same color as its built-in row icons, and sizes them 24 × 24 points by default.

  ---

  - `placement`
  - `UserProfileCustomRowPlacement`

  Where to insert the row relative to Clerk's built-in rows:

  - `.sectionStart(_:)` or `.sectionEnd(_:)`: The start or end of the `.profile` or `.account` section
  - `.before(_:)` or `.after(_:)`: Before or after a built-in row (`.manageAccount`, `.security`, `.switchAccount`, `.addAccount`, or `.signOut`)

  Defaults to `.sectionEnd(.profile)`.

  A row placed before or after a built-in row appears only when that built-in row does. `.switchAccount` and `.addAccount` appear only in [multi-session applications](/docs/guides/secure/session-options#multi-session-applications), and `.switchAccount` only when the user has more than one session. To always show a row in the account section, use `.sectionStart(.account)` or `.sectionEnd(.account)`.

  ---

  - `route`
  - `Route: Hashable`

  The value passed to `userProfileDestination(_:)`, or pushed onto your `navigationPath`, when the user selects the row.

  ---

  - `title`
  - `LocalizedStringKey | String`

  The row's title.
</Properties>

### `OAuthProviderConfig`

<Properties>
  - `additionalScopes`
  - `[String]`

  Additional OAuth scopes to request from the provider. Defaults to `[]`.

  ---

  - `prompts`
  - `[OIDCPrompt]`

  OpenID Connect prompts to send to the provider: `.none`, `.login`, `.consent`, or `.selectAccount`. Defaults to `[]`.

  ---

  - `provider`
  - `OAuthProvider`

  The provider the configuration applies to, such as `.google`.
</Properties>
