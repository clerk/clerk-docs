The WebView keeps its cookies when the user signs out of your app. Clerk ends the web sessions that the app's links created once the app's session ends, except in the cases listed under [Sign-out](/docs/guides/development/native-to-web#sign-out). When the WebView still holds the previous user's web session, the hook fails with the `other_user_signed_in` error code.

To start each user with a clean WebView, render it with the `incognito` prop:

- On iOS, the WebView keeps its cookies in memory only, and discards them when it unmounts. Don't combine `incognito` with `sharedCookiesEnabled`, which copies cookies between the WebView and your app's shared cookie storage.
- On Android, the WebView clears cookies when it mounts. Android WebViews share one cookie store, so this can sign users out of other WebViews in your app that rely on those cookies. The cookies stay after the WebView unmounts, until the next clear.

If your WebView needs to keep cookies between visits, or the cookies must not stay on an Android device after sign-out, clear them with a cookie manager such as [`@react-native-cookies/cookies`](https://github.com/react-native-cookies/cookies) when the user signs out or a different user signs in. On iOS, call `clearAll(true)` to clear the WebView's cookies, and also `clearAll()` if you set `sharedCookiesEnabled`, which copies cookies into your app's shared cookie storage. Both `clearAll(true)` on iOS and `clearAll()` on Android clear every cookie that your WebViews share, which can sign users out of other WebViews in your app. Wait for the clear to finish before you show the WebView to the next user, or call `retry()` after it finishes.
