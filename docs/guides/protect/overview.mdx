Protect is Clerk's fraud and abuse protection system, built into every sign-up and sign-in. It evaluates each attempt using signals from multiple sources and then allows, challenges, or blocks the attempt based on the protections you've enabled.

Because Protect runs within Clerk's authentication flows, applications using Clerk's prebuilt components or the Account Portal need no changes to their sign-up flow once they meet the [requirements](/docs/guides/protect/getting-started#requirements).

## Features

- **[Protections](/docs/guides/protect/protections/overview)** covers defenses you manage on the **Protections** page in the Clerk Dashboard, such as blocks on sign-ups from VPNs or disposable email addresses.
- **[Sign-in security](/docs/guides/protect/sign-in-security/overview)** covers settings you manage on the **Sign-in security** page in the Clerk Dashboard: [Device Trust](/docs/guides/protect/device-trust), [account lockout after failed sign-ins](/docs/guides/protect/user-lockout), and [user enumeration protection](/docs/guides/protect/user-enumeration-protection).
- **[Application Logs](/docs/guides/dashboard/logs/application-logs#protect-events)** let you inspect Protect events, including blocked and challenged attempts.

## Plans and pricing

Every application includes basic protections at no extra cost, such as challenges for suspicious sign-ups and blocks on disposable email addresses. If you're under frequent attack, the Business plan includes advanced protections, such as rate limits, network-type blocks, and country restrictions.
