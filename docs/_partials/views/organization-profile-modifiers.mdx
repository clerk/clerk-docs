<Properties>
  - `organizationProfileDestination(_:)`
  - `@ViewBuilder (Route) -> some View`

  Builds the view to push when the user selects a custom row. The closure receives the selected row's `route`.

  ---

  - `organizationProfileRows(_:)`
  - [`[OrganizationProfileCustomRow<Route>]`](#organizationprofilecustomrow)

  Custom rows to show on the root Organization profile screen, alongside Clerk's built-in rows. Rows that share a placement show in array order.
</Properties>

### `OrganizationProfileCustomRow`

<Properties>
  - `icon`
  - `OrganizationProfileRowIcon`

  The row's icon. Pass `.system(name:)` for an SF Symbol, or `.asset(name:width:height:)` for an image in your app's asset catalog. Clerk renders asset icons as templates, tinted the same color as its built-in row icons, and sizes them 24 × 24 points by default.

  ---

  - `placement`
  - `OrganizationProfileCustomRowPlacement`

  Where to insert the row relative to Clerk's built-in rows:

  - `.sectionStart(_:)` or `.sectionEnd(_:)`: The start or end of the `.profile` or `.actions` section
  - `.before(_:)` or `.after(_:)`: Before or after a built-in row (`.members`, `.verifiedDomains`, `.leaveOrganization`, or `.deleteOrganization`)

  Defaults to `.sectionEnd(.profile)`.

  A row placed before or after a built-in row appears only when that built-in row does. `.members`, `.verifiedDomains`, and `.deleteOrganization` depend on the user's Permissions and your instance settings, as listed in [Visible rows and Permissions](/docs/reference/views/organization/organization-profile-view#visible-rows-and-permissions). `.leaveOrganization` always appears. To always show a row, use `.sectionStart(_:)` or `.sectionEnd(_:)`.

  ---

  - `route`
  - `Route: Hashable`

  The value passed to `organizationProfileDestination(_:)`, or pushed onto your `navigationPath`, when the user selects the row.

  ---

  - `title`
  - `LocalizedStringKey | String`

  The row's title.
</Properties>
